## Ranger
Long range with extra long range skillshot projectile

## Archer
Should be healthier than ranger and slightly faster, also deal more damage.

## Goblin snatcher
Fast but low health and attack damage, except against buildings. 

# Bridge keeper
Medium speed caster that spawns an AOE projectile at the targets feet. Has two abilities; one that pushes enemies away and one that pulls them in.

# Ice man
Caster with AOE projectiles that slow. Activatable ability that temporarily stuns all slowed nearby enemies.
